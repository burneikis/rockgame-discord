# Gravitas Rules

> "Art you can play with." A stone stacking game handmade in Vermont.

These rules combine the published descriptions (Art of Play, Brattleboro
Reformer) with house clarifications. The official instruction card is not
available online.

## Components

- 1 stone base
- 1 six-sided die
- Stone pieces in six shapes, each matched to a die face:

| Die | Shape    |
|-----|----------|
| 1   | Circle   |
| 2   | Shaft    |
| 3   | Triangle |
| 4   | Square   |
| 5   | Pentagon |
| 6   | Cube     |

Set sizes:

- 2-player set: 12 stones
- 6-player set: 36 stones
- 2-player expansion: 12 more stones

## Setup

1. Put the base in the middle of the table.
2. Give each player one stone of every shape (6 stones each).
   - Tournament variant: two of every shape (12 stones each).
3. Pick any turn order. It doesn't matter who goes first, as long as players
   take turns one at a time.

## Your Turn

1. Roll the die.
2. Place stones from your supply whose numbers add up to the roll. Either:
   - the single matching stone, or
   - any combination of two or more stones that adds up to the roll.

   Example: if you roll a 5, you may place a pentagon (5), a circle (1) and a
   square (4), a shaft (2) and a triangle (3), or a circle (1), a circle (1)
   and a triangle (3) if you have two circles.

   You can end up with duplicate shapes by collecting stones that fall (see
   Falling Stones).
3. Place the stones one at a time. You may put each one **anywhere** on the
   base or the tower.
4. If you can place, you must. There is no passing.
5. **Can't play?** If no stone or combination in your supply adds up to the
   roll, you must pick up one stone from the tower and add it to your supply.
   - You may take **any** stone, even one from the bottom of the tower.
   - Any stones that fall while you do this are yours too.
   - Your turn ends once you have picked up the stone and the tower has
     settled.

## Falling Stones

If a stone falls off the tower, or the whole tower falls, while you are
placing or picking up:

- You take back **every stone that fell**, including stones that belong to
  other players.
- Stones that stay on the tower stay where they are.
- Your turn ends immediately, even if you had stones left to place.

## Winning

The first player to have no stones left wins. You win as soon as your last
stone is placed and nothing falls.
